# The New Collection

The New Collection was our annually published MCR journal. It was peer-reviewed, ISSN-accredited, and accepted submissions from any field. It also had a non-peer reviewed section, focusing on topics of concern at New College. The journal is no longer published but we encourage you to go through previous issues of The New Collection on this page.
